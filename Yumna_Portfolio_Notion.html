Case Study 01 — Personal Project

FinPilot – Predictive Financial Guardrails

A fintech product concept that uses calendar signals and spending data to help users make smarter financial decisions before they overspend.

🏦 Fintech 📱 Mobile App 👤 Solo PM Work 🔍 0→1 Product
The Problem

Most budgeting apps tell users what went wrong after they've already overspent. There is no product that proactively warns users about upcoming high-spend periods — rent cycles, festive seasons, travel — before the damage is done. Users don't need more dashboards. They need a financial early warning system.

User Research & Personas
  • The Reactive Spender — Earns well, but consistently surprised by month-end balance. Wants warnings, not reports.
  • The First-Time Earner — Young professional, no financial habits formed. Needs guardrails, not complexity.
  • The Planner — Has goals but struggles to connect daily decisions to long-term outcomes.
The Solution

FinPilot syncs with a user's calendar and spending history to predict upcoming financial pressure points. It sends proactive nudges like: "Your rent is due in 5 days and you've spent 80% of this month's discretionary budget." Key features include a Spend Forecast Timeline, Smart Calendar Alerts, and a Financial Health Score that updates in real time.

Prioritisation & Tradeoffs
  • Prioritised proactive nudges over retrospective dashboards — insight before damage
  • Chose calendar integration over bank statement parsing for faster onboarding
  • Deferred social/community features to v2 — focus on solo utility first
  • Kept onboarding to under 3 steps to reduce drop-off at signup
Competitive Landscape

Benchmarked against Walnut, CRED, Jupiter, and Groww. Key gap identified: all competitors are reactive by design — they show you what happened. FinPilot's differentiator is prediction, not reporting. No direct competitor in India occupies this proactive-guardrail space.

Success Metrics
  • % of users who acted on a nudge before overspending (target: 40% within 30 days)
  • Reduction in month-end budget overrun vs baseline
  • D7 and D30 retention — stickiness of proactive nudge habit
  • NPS and qualitative feedback on "financial confidence" post-use
Key Learnings
"The biggest insight was that users don't want more data — they want less anxiety. FinPilot's job isn't to inform, it's to reduce the cognitive load of managing money. That reframe changed every product decision I made."
Case Study 02 — ICICI Lombard

TripSecure – End-to-End Digital Claim Journey

Designed and launched a seamless digital claim intimation experience for TripSecure travel insurance across mobile, web, and M-view platforms.

🛡️ Insurtech 📱 Mobile + Web 👥 Cross-functional 🚀 0→1 Launch
The Problem

When TripSecure policyholders faced an emergency abroad — a medical issue, trip cancellation, or lost baggage — the claim filing process was entirely offline. Users had to call a helpline, wait on hold, and navigate a manual process during an already stressful moment. This led to high drop-offs, poor NPS scores at the claim stage, and significant ops overhead for the support team.

My Role
  • Collaborated with PMs to define functional requirements and feature specifications
  • Worked directly with designers on user journey flows and UI components
  • Coordinated with QA teams across 3 platforms: Mobile (Flutter), Web, and M-View
  • Implemented Firebase Analytics to track user behavior and identify drop-off points
  • Contributed to release planning and end-to-end launch execution
The Solution

A fully digital, self-serve claim intimation journey built across all three platforms. Users could now initiate a claim in under 5 minutes — upload documents, select claim type, track status — all without calling support. The journey was designed for high-stress, low-patience users in unfamiliar environments, prioritising clarity and speed above everything else.

Key Product Decisions
  • Designed for one-handed mobile use — most claims initiated mid-travel on phones
  • Progressive disclosure — showed only what was needed at each step, not the full form upfront
  • Offline-tolerant design — auto-saved progress for users with poor connectivity abroad
  • Localised copy — plain language over insurance jargon at every touchpoint
